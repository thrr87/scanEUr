# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

scanEUr is an open-source project that deals with sensitive topics: vendors, privacy, jurisdiction, infrastructure, and trust. Contributors are expected to communicate carefully, respectfully, and evidence-first.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- Treat others with respect.
- Assume good faith where reasonable.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and wording rather than people.
- Use precise language.
- Avoid inflammatory claims about vendors, countries, companies, or contributors.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when relevant.
- Welcome corrections.
- Keep discussions focused on project goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Spam or promotional abuse.
- Undisclosed paid influence over vendor data or recommendations.
- Attempts to pressure maintainers into paid placement, hidden ranking, or unsupported claims.
- Repeated legal or vendor claims without evidence after correction.

## Vendor discussions

Vendor discussions must be evidence-based. The project should not host broad ideological attacks. It is acceptable to discuss detection patterns, documentation, ownership/control evidence, data-processing signals, migration effort, and alternatives. It is not acceptable to make unsupported claims that a vendor is illegal, unsafe, or compliant.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may:

- Ask participants to edit wording.
- Close discussions that are unproductive or abusive.
- Remove comments or pull requests that violate this code.
- Temporarily or permanently block participants for serious or repeated violations.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers using the project’s documented contact channel. Security issues should follow `SECURITY.md`.

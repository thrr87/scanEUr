# Funding

scanEUr may accept funding only when it does not influence recommendations, vendor inclusion, scoring, ranking, or methodology.

## Acceptable funding models

Acceptable funding may include:

- GitHub Sponsors.
- Open Collective.
- Grants.
- Donations.
- Transparent infrastructure sponsorship that does not influence scoring or recommendations.

## Forbidden funding models

The project must not accept funding through:

- Affiliate links.
- Paid placement.
- Paid ranking.
- Sponsored recommendations.
- Monetized alternatives lists.
- Vendor payments to suppress or improve findings.

## Independence requirement

Funding must not influence:

- Vendor profiles.
- Verification status.
- Detection rules.
- Scoring.
- Recommendations.
- Alternatives ranking.
- Report wording.
- Dispute outcomes.

## Transparency

Material funding relationships should be disclosed publicly. Maintainers and contributors should disclose conflicts of interest when working on related vendor data or methodology.

## Principle

scanEUr should optimize for maximum trust over monetisation.
